Keeping your inventory accurate is one of the most important parts of running a trustworthy store on Demat. When buyers see a product listed, they expect it to be available — running out of stock mid-order creates friction, delays, and negative reviews. Demat's inventory tools make it straightforward to track quantities, manage variants, and stay on top of restocking before you sell out.

## Stock Tracking

When you create a product listing, you set the initial stock quantity for that item. From that point on, Demat automatically decrements the stock count every time an order is placed — so your available quantity stays accurate without any manual tracking on your end.

<Note>
  Products with zero stock are automatically hidden from marketplace listings until you restock them. Buyers won't see out-of-stock items, which protects your store's reputation and prevents frustrated orders.
</Note>

---

## Update Inventory Quantities

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Products">
    Open your dashboard and navigate to **My Store → Products** to see your full product list with current stock levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the product to edit">
    Click on the product whose quantity you want to update to open its edit page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the Quantity field">
    Find the **Quantity** field in the Inventory section and enter the updated stock count. If you have variants, update each variant's quantity individually (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the updated quantity. The new stock level takes effect immediately on your live listing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Low Stock Alerts

Demat monitors your stock levels and sends you a notification when a product is running low — before it hits zero. This gives you time to reorder or restock without losing sales or disappointing buyers.

You'll receive low-stock alerts in your Demat notification center. Make sure your in-app notifications are enabled so you don't miss them.

## Inventory Sync (Premium)

For high-volume sellers managing large catalogs with frequent order activity, the Premium plan includes **inventory sync** — a feature that keeps stock counts accurate across many simultaneous orders. This is particularly valuable during promotions, flash sales, or periods of high demand when multiple buyers may be purchasing the same item at the same time.

Inventory sync reduces the risk of overselling by ensuring that stock decrements happen in real time as orders are confirmed.

## Managing Variants

When a product comes in multiple options — such as sizes, colors, or configurations — each variant has its own independent stock count. This means you can accurately reflect that you have 10 units in size M but only 2 in size XL, for example.

To update variant-level quantities:

1. Open the product's edit page from **My Store → Products**
2. Scroll to the **Variants** section
3. Click into each variant and update its **Quantity** field individually
4. Save your changes

Keeping variant quantities accurate is especially important for Fashion, Beauty, and Houseware listings where specific options sell at different rates.

<Tip>
  Use the bulk CSV export (Premium) to audit your full inventory offline. Download your product catalog, review and update quantities in a spreadsheet, then re-import the file to apply all changes at once — much faster than editing products one by one.
</Tip>
