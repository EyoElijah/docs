Your Demat storefront is your public-facing shop on the marketplace — a dedicated space where buyers can browse your products, read about your business, and place orders. Setting it up takes only a few minutes, and every plan — including Free — gives you a unique store link you can share anywhere.

## Create and Configure Your Store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register or log in to your Demat account">
    Visit [demat.shop](https://demat.shop) and sign in. If you don't have an account yet, complete the registration flow to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Settings">
    From your dashboard, navigate to **My Store**, then open the **Settings** tab to access your storefront configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your store name">
    Choose a store name — this becomes your permanent store link at **demat.shop/[storename]**. Pick something short, memorable, and reflective of your brand.

    <Note>
      Store links are unique across the platform. Once you claim a name, no other seller can use it — so choose a name that's easy to remember and share.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a profile photo and store banner">
    Upload a clear profile photo (your logo or a professional headshot) and a banner image that represents your store. These visuals are the first thing buyers see when they visit your page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a store description">
    Tell buyers who you are, what you sell, and why they should shop with you. A clear, confident description builds trust and helps buyers understand your offering at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your store category">
    Select the category that best fits your primary inventory — such as Fashion, Gadgets, Beauty, or Services. This helps Demat surface your store to the right buyers in the marketplace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share your link">
    Click **Save** to publish your storefront. Your store is now live at **demat.shop/[storename]** and ready to receive visitors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Store Design (Basic & Premium)

Basic and Premium plan sellers unlock full store design customization. You can change your store's color palette, adjust the layout, and apply branding elements — fonts, accent colors, and section arrangements — to create a shopping experience that feels consistent with your business identity.

To access design settings, go to **My Store → Settings → Store Design** and apply your preferred theme. Changes are previewed in real time before you save.

---

## SEO for Your Store (Basic & Premium)

The Basic plan includes built-in SEO tools that help your store appear in Google and other search engine results. When buyers search for products you sell, your store has a chance to show up organically — bringing you traffic without paid advertising.

To optimize your store for search:
- Use clear, descriptive product titles and descriptions
- Fill in your store's meta title and description under **My Store → Settings → SEO**
- Keep your store name and category relevant to what you actually sell

<Tip>
  Share your store link on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and other social platforms to drive traffic. Your demat.shop link works like any website URL — paste it anywhere.
</Tip>
