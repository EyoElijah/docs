Adding products to your Demat store is how you turn your storefront into a working shop. Whether you're listing a single item or building a full catalog, Demat gives you the tools to present your products clearly, manage stock accurately, and reach buyers across the marketplace. The steps below walk you through creating your first listing and scaling up from there.

## Add a Single Product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Products → Add Product">
    Open your dashboard, navigate to **My Store**, click **Products**, and then select **Add Product** to open the listing form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload product photos">
    Add clear photos from multiple angles — front, back, close-up, and in-use where relevant. High-quality images are one of the strongest signals of a trustworthy listing.

    <Tip>
      High-quality photos significantly increase buyer trust and conversion. Use natural light and a clean background to make your product stand out.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a product title and description">
    Write a specific, accurate title and a detailed description. Include materials, dimensions, condition, and any other details a buyer would want to know before purchasing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your price in ₦">
    Enter your selling price in Nigerian Naira. You can also enable the option to accept offers if you're open to negotiation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Select the most relevant category for your product — Fashion, Gadgets, Beauty, Houseware, Automobile, Thrift, or others. The right category ensures buyers browsing that section can discover your listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add product variants (Basic & Premium)">
    If your product comes in multiple sizes, colors, or configurations, add each as a variant. Each variant can have its own price and stock quantity, so buyers can select exactly what they want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set stock quantity">
    Enter how many units you have available. Demat tracks inventory automatically and decrements stock as orders come in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the listing">
    Click **Publish** to make the product live on your store and visible in the marketplace. You can also save as a draft if you're not ready to go live yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Product Limits by Plan

The number of products you can list depends on your current plan.

| Plan    | Product Limit             |
|---------|---------------------------|
| Free    | Individual listings only  |
| Basic   | Up to 100 products        |
| Premium | Unlimited                 |

## Add Product Videos (Premium)

Premium plan sellers can upload a short video alongside product photos. Videos let you demonstrate fit, scale, functionality, or quality in ways that still images can't — making your listings more compelling and reducing buyer hesitation.

To add a video, open the product edit page and upload your file in the **Media** section alongside your photos.

## Bulk Import & Export (Premium)

If you have a large catalog, adding products one by one is impractical. Premium sellers can import an entire product catalog from a CSV or Excel file in a single operation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Products → Import">
    Open the **Products** section in your dashboard and click the **Import** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the template CSV">
    Download the official Demat import template. It contains all the required columns — title, price, category, stock quantity, variants, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your product details">
    Open the template in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app. Add one product per row, filling in every required column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the completed file">
    Return to the Import screen and upload your filled-in CSV or Excel file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm the import">
    Demat previews the products parsed from your file. Check for any errors, make corrections if needed, and click **Confirm Import** to publish the full batch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also **export** your product catalog at any time — go to **My Store → Products → Export** to download a CSV of all your listings. Use this for offline analysis, price updates, or restocking audits.

## AI-Powered Recommendations (Basic & Premium)

Demat uses AI to analyze buyer behavior and surface your products to shoppers most likely to be interested in them. When a buyer browses the marketplace or views a related listing, Demat's recommendation engine can highlight your products — increasing visibility without any extra effort on your part.

This feature is active automatically for Basic and Premium plan sellers. Keeping your product titles, descriptions, and categories accurate improves the quality of recommendations your listings receive.
