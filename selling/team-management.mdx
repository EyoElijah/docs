As your business grows, managing your Demat store alone becomes harder to scale. The Premium plan lets you add team members to your store account so you can delegate order fulfillment, customer communication, and inventory updates without sharing your personal login credentials. Each staff member gets their own access, and you control exactly what they can see and do.

<Note>
  Team management is a **Premium plan** feature. Upgrade your plan to unlock staff accounts and role-based access for your store.
</Note>

## Adding Staff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Team → Add Staff">
    Open your dashboard and navigate to **My Store → Team**, then click **Add Staff** to open the invitation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the staff member's email address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to invite. They must have a valid email to receive the invitation and create their own login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select the role that matches what you want this person to be able to do. Typical roles include:

    <Accordion title="Store Manager">
      Full access to store operations — products, orders, customer communication, and store settings. Suitable for a trusted business partner or senior employee.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Order Fulfillment">
      Access to the Orders section to view, accept, and update order status. Cannot change store settings or view financial data. Ideal for staff handling packing and dispatch.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Inventory Staff">
      Access to the Products section to update stock levels and product details. Cannot process orders or access financial information. Best for warehouse or stock management staff.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Customer Support">
      Access to in-app chat and order communication to respond to buyer inquiries. Limited to customer-facing functions only.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The staff member will receive an email with a link to accept the invite and set up their own Demat login credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff member accesses the store">
    Once they accept the invitation and log in, they'll see your store dashboard with access limited to the permissions their role allows. They work under your store account but with their own secure login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Role-Based Access

Not every team member needs access to everything. Role-based access ensures that sensitive areas of your store — financial data, payout settings, and store configuration — are only accessible to the people who truly need them.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What staff can access">
    Access depends on the assigned role. Most staff roles can:
    - View and manage orders within their scope
    - Communicate with buyers via in-app chat
    - Update product listings or inventory quantities
    - View store performance relevant to their function
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="What is restricted">
    By default, non-owner roles cannot:
    - Change store-level settings or store name
    - Access wallet balances, payout history, or banking details
    - Add or remove other team members
    - Upgrade or downgrade the store's plan
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Give staff only the access they need for their specific role. Limiting financial and settings access to store owners and managers reduces the risk of accidental changes and keeps your business data secure.
</Tip>

## Removing a Team Member

If a staff member leaves your business or no longer needs access, remove them immediately to protect your store.

1. Go to **My Store → Team**
2. Find the staff member in your team list
3. Click **Remove** next to their name
4. Confirm the action

Their access is revoked immediately — they will no longer be able to log in to your store dashboard.

<Warning>
  Removing a staff member does not delete any actions they took while active (such as orders they processed or messages they sent). Only their future access is revoked.
</Warning>
