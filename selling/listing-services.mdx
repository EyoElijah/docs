Demat isn't just for physical products — it's a full marketplace for service providers too. Whether you're a consultant, photographer, tutor, beauty professional, event planner, or tech support specialist, you can list your services, set your schedule, and accept client bookings directly through the platform. Payments are held in escrow and released only after the client confirms the service was delivered, so both sides of every transaction are protected.

## Create a Service Listing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Services → Add Service">
    Open your dashboard and navigate to **My Store → Services**, then click **Add Service** to open the service listing form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your service and write a description">
    Give your service a clear, specific title — for example, "90-Minute Portrait Photography Session" or "1-Hour Python Tutoring Class." Write a detailed description that explains exactly what the client will receive, what's included, and any requirements they need to know upfront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your pricing">
    Choose a pricing model that fits your service:
    - **Fixed price** — one flat rate for the entire service
    - **Per hour** — a rate applied to the number of hours booked

    Enter your price in ₦.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability">
    Choose the days of the week and time slots when you're available to take bookings. Clients will only be able to schedule appointments within the windows you define.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure booking settings">
    Set how much advance notice you require before a booking (for example, at least 24 hours), and cap the maximum number of bookings you accept per day to avoid overcommitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your service listing">
    Click **Publish** to make your service live on the marketplace. Clients can now find and book you directly through your store page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Bookings

Once your service is live, here's how the booking flow works from your side:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive a booking notification">
    When a client books your service, you'll get an in-app notification with the details — date, time, and any notes the client included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and respond">
    Open the booking request and choose to **Confirm** or **Decline** it. If the time no longer works, you can also message the client to propose an alternative slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat with the client">
    Use Demat's in-app chat to confirm logistics — meeting point, call link, materials needed, or any other pre-service details.

    <Tip>
      Keep your availability calendar up to date to avoid double-bookings. A conflict-free schedule helps you maintain a strong rating and encourages repeat clients.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver the service">
    Provide the service as agreed. Make sure the scope matches what you listed and what the client booked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client confirms — payment releases">
    After delivery, the client confirms the service was completed. Once they confirm, the payment held in escrow is released to your wallet, minus the 3% seller fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Service payments use the same escrow system as product transactions. The client's payment is held securely until they confirm the service was delivered — protecting both you and the client from disputes.
</Info>
