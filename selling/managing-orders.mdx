Every sale on Demat moves through a clear, protected order flow. From the moment a buyer places an order to the point funds land in your wallet, the process is designed to keep both parties secure and informed. Understanding how orders work — and how to handle them efficiently — helps you build a strong seller reputation and get paid faster.

## Order Flow

When a buyer purchases from your store, here's what happens at each stage:

1. **Buyer places an order** — the buyer submits the order and their payment is immediately moved into escrow.
2. **Funds held in escrow** — the payment is secured and held by Demat. You don't receive it yet, but it's confirmed and waiting.
3. **You receive a notification** — Demat notifies you of the new order so you can act promptly.
4. **You accept and fulfill** — you confirm the order, prepare the item, and arrange handover with the buyer.
5. **Buyer confirms receipt** — after receiving the item, the buyer marks the order as delivered.
6. **Escrow releases funds** — the payment is released from escrow to your Demat wallet, minus the 3% seller fee.

## Handle an Order Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Store → Orders">
    Open your dashboard and navigate to **My Store → Orders** to see all incoming and active orders in one view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review new orders and offers">
    Check each new order or offer. You'll see the item, quantity, buyer's offered price (if they submitted an offer), and any notes they included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept, reject, or counter an offer">
    For standard orders, click **Accept** to confirm. If the buyer sent an offer below your listed price, choose to accept their price, reject the offer, or send a counter-offer with your preferred amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat with the buyer">
    Use in-app chat to coordinate pickup or delivery — agree on a time, location, or courier arrangement. Keep all communication on-platform so there's a clear record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare and hand over the item">
    Get the item ready and deliver it to the buyer through the agreed method — in-person pickup, third-party courier, or direct delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as Delivered">
    Once the item is handed over, go to the order in your dashboard and click **Mark as Delivered**. This notifies the buyer to confirm receipt.

    <Warning>
      Once you mark an order as Delivered, the buyer has a window to confirm receipt or raise a dispute. Ensure the item matches its listing description exactly — condition, quantity, and any included accessories — before marking delivery.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for buyer confirmation">
    The buyer has up to 48–72 hours to confirm receipt. If they don't respond within that window, the order may be auto-confirmed depending on platform settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your payout">
    Once the buyer confirms, the escrowed funds are released to your Demat wallet minus the **3% seller fee**. You can then withdraw your balance at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Offers and Negotiations

Buyers on Demat can submit offers below your listed price — this is intentional. The marketplace is designed to support negotiation, particularly for higher-value items in categories like Property, Automobiles, and Gadgets.

When you receive an offer:
- **Accept** — the order proceeds at the buyer's offered price
- **Reject** — the offer is declined and the buyer can relist or move on
- **Counter** — you propose a different price; the buyer can then accept or respond

Use in-app chat alongside the offer system to discuss terms, bundle deals, or clarify product details before committing.

## Downloadable Invoices (Premium)

Premium sellers can download a PDF invoice for every completed order directly from the order detail page. Invoices include the buyer's details, item breakdown, total amount, and transaction date — making them useful for bookkeeping, accounting reconciliation, and VAT records.

To download an invoice, go to **My Store → Orders**, open a completed order, and click **Download Invoice**.

---

## Export Order Data (Premium)

Premium plan sellers can export their full order history to CSV or Excel. This is useful for offline analysis, reconciling earnings, tracking fulfillment performance, or sharing sales data with an accountant.

To export, go to **My Store → Orders → Export** and choose your date range and file format.

<Info>
  Your sales analytics dashboard — visible from the main dashboard — gives you a real-time overview of revenue, order volume, and top-performing products without needing to export.
</Info>
