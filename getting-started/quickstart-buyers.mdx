Demat makes it safe and simple to shop from sellers across Nigeria and Africa. Every transaction is backed by escrow — your money is held securely until you confirm you've received your item exactly as described. This guide walks you through everything from finding a product to leaving your first review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [demat.shop](https://demat.shop) and sign up for a free account. The process takes under two minutes — all you need is an email address and a password. See [Create Your Demat Account](/getting-started/create-account) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse the marketplace">
    Go to the **Marketplace** tab to explore thousands of listings. Products and services are organised into eight categories to help you find exactly what you need:

    - **Fashion** — clothing, footwear, and accessories
    - **Gadgets** — phones, laptops, electronics, and accessories
    - **Beauty** — skincare, haircare, and cosmetics
    - **Property** — rentals, sales, and commercial spaces
    - **Automobile** — cars, bikes, spare parts, and accessories
    - **Houseware** — furniture, appliances, and home decor
    - **Services** — freelance, professional, and skilled services
    - **Thrift** — fairly-used items at discounted prices

    Use the search bar or filter by location to narrow your results.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find a product you like">
    Click any listing to open the full product page. Here you can review the **seller's store**, **product description**, **price**, **photos**, and the **distance** between you and the seller's location — useful for planning pickup or estimating delivery time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    Before placing an order, you need a funded Demat wallet. Go to **Wallet → Fund Wallet** and deposit money via **Paystack bank transfer**. A **1% deposit fee** applies, capped at a maximum of **₦300** per transaction. Your balance reflects immediately once the transfer is confirmed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place your order">
    On the product page, click **Buy Now** to purchase at the listed price, or click **Make Offer** to propose a different amount and negotiate with the seller. Once the order is confirmed, your funds move into **escrow** — they are held safely and are not released to the seller until you confirm delivery.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chat with the seller">
    Use the **in-app chat** to communicate directly with the seller. You can ask questions about the item, negotiate the final price, and coordinate a pickup location or delivery arrangement — all without leaving the Demat app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm delivery">
    When you receive your item and are satisfied with it, open the order in your dashboard and tap **Confirm Delivery**. This releases the escrowed funds to the seller. If there is a problem with the item, you can raise a **dispute** instead — Demat resolves disputes within **7 business days** and processes approved refunds within **3–5 business days**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rate your experience">
    After delivery is confirmed, leave a **star rating and written review** on the seller's store page. Your feedback helps other buyers make informed decisions and rewards trustworthy sellers with better visibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You typically have **48–72 hours** to confirm or reject a delivery after it is marked as dispatched. If you take no action within that window, the platform may automatically release the escrowed funds to the seller per Demat's auto-release policy — so act promptly if you have concerns.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Never pay a seller outside the Demat platform** — by bank transfer, mobile money, or any other method. Escrow protection applies **only to in-app transactions**. Payments made outside Demat cannot be recovered if something goes wrong.
</Warning>
