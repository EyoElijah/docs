You can start selling on Demat in under five minutes — no business registration, no paperwork, and no upfront fees required. Whether you're an individual selling thrift items or a brand with a full product catalogue, your store is ready the moment you create an account. This guide takes you from zero to your first payout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up for a free Demat account at [demat.shop](https://demat.shop). All you need is an email address and a password. Your storefront is created automatically and goes live immediately. See [Create Your Demat Account](/getting-started/create-account) for a detailed walkthrough.
  </Step>

  <Step title="List your first product">
    In your dashboard, navigate to **My Store → Add Product**. Fill in the following details to create your listing:

    - **Title** — a clear, searchable product name
    - **Photos** — high-quality images from multiple angles
    - **Price** — set in Nigerian Naira (₦)
    - **Description** — include condition, size, specifications, and any other relevant details
    - **Category** — choose from Fashion, Gadgets, Beauty, Property, Automobile, Houseware, Services, or Thrift

    Click **Publish** and your product appears in the marketplace instantly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your store link">
    Your store is live at:

    ```
    demat.shop/[storename]
    ```

    Share this link on **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**, **Twitter/X**, or any other channel to drive traffic directly to your storefront. Every product you add is visible to anyone who visits your link — no app download required for browsing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive an order">
    When a buyer places an order, you'll receive an **in-app notification**. The buyer's payment moves into **escrow** immediately — meaning the funds are secured and awaiting release to you, but the buyer's money is committed and cannot be quietly withdrawn.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chat and arrange delivery">
    Open the order and use the **in-app chat** to message the buyer. Confirm the item details, agree on a pickup location or delivery method, and keep all communication inside the app so every conversation is on record if a dispute arises.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fulfill the order">
    Hand over the item to the buyer — in person or via a delivery service. Once the item has been dispatched or handed over, open the order in your dashboard and tap **Mark as Delivered** to notify the buyer and start the delivery confirmation window.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get paid">
    When the buyer confirms receipt, the escrowed funds are released to your **Demat wallet** automatically. A **3% seller fee** is deducted from the payout amount. For example, on a ₦10,000 order you receive ₦9,700 into your wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Withdraw your earnings">
    Go to **Wallet → Withdraw** and transfer your balance to any Nigerian bank account. Withdrawal fees are tiered by amount:

    | Amount | Fee |
    |---|---|
    | ₦5,000 and below | ₦10 |
    | ₦5,001 – ₦50,000 | ₦25 |
    | Above ₦50,000 | ₦50 |

    Funds typically arrive in your bank account within minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
