Whether you want to buy from thousands of African sellers or open your own storefront, creating a Demat account is your first step. This page walks you through registration, profile setup, wallet activation, and finding your unique store link so you can start buying or selling right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit demat.shop and click Sign Up">
    Open [demat.shop](https://demat.shop) in your browser. On the homepage, click the **Sign Up** button in the top navigation to open the registration form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email and create a password">
    Fill in your email address and choose a strong password. Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then tick the agreement checkbox and click **Create Account** to submit your registration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Demat. Click the confirmation link inside to activate your account. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder or request a new verification email from the login screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    After signing in for the first time, add your **display name** so buyers and sellers can identify you. You can also upload a **profile photo** to build trust — this step is optional but recommended for sellers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate your wallet">
    Go to **Wallet → Activate Wallet** and enter your Bank Verification Number (BVN) to unlock funding and withdrawal features. Your NIN can also be provided for additional identity verification, though it is not required at this stage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your BVN is encrypted in transit and at rest in compliance with Nigeria's **NDPR regulations**. Demat never shares your BVN with third parties or displays it after submission.
</Note>

<Info>
  You don't need to activate your wallet immediately. Feel free to browse the marketplace and set up your store first — wallet activation is only required when you're ready to fund your account or withdraw earnings.
</Info>

## Your Store Link

The moment your account is created, Demat generates a live storefront for you at:

```
demat.shop/[storename]
```

This link is ready to share right away. Post it in your **WhatsApp status**, add it to your **Instagram bio**, drop it in **Twitter/X**, or include it on a business card — anyone who taps the link lands directly on your personal store page. You can customise your store's name, banner, and product listings at any time from your seller dashboard.
