## A marketplace built on trust

Browse stores, discover products, and transact with confidence all protected by our escrow system.

### Escrow Protection

Your money stays safe in escrow until you receive and confirm your item. No more worrying about scams — Demat holds the payment securely until the buyer is satisfied.

## Everything you need to grow online

Whether you're a solo seller or a growing business, Demat gives you powerful tools to succeed.

### Shareable Storefront

Get a unique link to your branded store. Share it on social media, WhatsApp, or anywhere to drive customers directly to you.

### Team Management

Add staff members to your business account with role-based access. Collaborate and manage your store as a team.

### Inventory Management

Track stock levels, manage products, and get alerts when items are running low.

### Sales Analytics

Understand your business with real-time dashboards, revenue tracking, and customer insights.

### Notes & Reminders

Create business notes, set reminders for follow-ups, restocking, or important tasks never miss a beat.

### Appointments

Service providers can accept and manage appointment bookings directly from their storefront. Clients book, you confirm.

### Secure Payments

Built-in wallet system with escrow protection. Fund, withdraw, and track every transaction.

### Trusted Marketplace

Verified sellers, buyer protection, and transparent reviews build trust in every transaction.

### Instant Setup

No coding needed. Go from sign-up to your first sale in under 5 minutes.
