About Demat

# eCommerce built for safer everyday selling

Demat is a modern marketplace where individuals and businesses can list products, manage storefronts, chat with buyers, and complete transactions through an escrow-backed payment flow. The goal is simple: make online selling easier to start and harder to exploit.

Mission and value

## Demat helps people sell with more structure and less risk

We built Demat for the real conditions of modern commerce: individuals selling occasionally, growing businesses needing better tools, and buyers who want more confidence before paying. The product combines marketplace discovery, direct communication, and protected payments into one experience.

### Our mission

Remove the friction that makes online selling feel confusing, unsafe, or too technical for everyday users.

### Our value

Trust, flexibility, and clarity. Sellers get room to grow, while buyers get a transaction process designed to feel safer.

What Demat does

Why people trust Demat

## Selling should feel clear from first chat to final payout

Demat reduces the guesswork in digital selling by combining marketplace discovery, protected payments, and direct buyer communication in one flow.

### Escrow-first checkout

Payments are held securely until the buyer confirms satisfaction, reducing fraud and disputes.

### Built-in conversation

Buyers and sellers can chat directly on Demat to ask questions and agree on pickup details.

### Flexible selling model

From single-item listings to managed storefronts, Demat supports sellers at different stages.
