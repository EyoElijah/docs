Privacy Policy for Demat Shop

Demat Shop is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is processed in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with our services.

---

### 1\. Definitions

- **Personal Data:** Any information that identifies you directly or indirectly, such as your name, email, BVN, phone number, etc.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
- **Data Subject:** An individual whose personal data is collected and processed.
- **Consent:**A freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of your wishes, signifying agreement to the processing of personal data.
- **Legitimate Interest:** A lawful basis for processing where necessary for the functioning of our business, including fraud prevention, service improvement, and dispute resolution.

### 3\. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Identification Data | Full name, phone number, email address, gender, BVN\* |
| Transaction Data | Wallet balance, payment method, transaction/order history |
| Escrow & Refund Data | Order details, cancellation reasons, supporting documentation |
| KYC & Compliance Data | Government-issued ID, utility bill, address verification |
| Technical Data | IP address, device details, browser type, login activity |
| Communication Data | Support requests, chat logs, user feedback |

**Note:** Your BVN and KYC documents are treated as sensitive personal data. These are encrypted, stored securely, and only collected where necessary for regulatory compliance.

### 4\. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data for the purposes outlined below:

| Purpose | Type of Data | Legal Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account creation | Identification Data | Consent; Performance of contract |
| Wallet transactions | Transaction Data | Performance of contract |
| Order fulfillment & escrow | Transaction, Escrow Data | Performance of contract |
| Refunds & dispute resolution | Escrow, Communication Data | Legitimate Interest (e.g., fraud prevention) |
| Customer support | Communication Data | Consent; Legitimate Interest (service quality) |
| KYC/AML compliance | KYC Data | Legal Obligation |
| Marketing (optional) | Identification, Communication Data | Consent |
| Analytics & service improvement | Technical Data | Legitimate Interest (platform optimization) |

For more information on the legal bases used, feel free to contact our DPO.

### 5\. Order Cancellation and Refund Process

You may cancel an order through the Demat Shop app by providing a valid reason (e.g., change of mind, incorrect order). If approved, any escrowed funds will be returned to your wallet within 3–7 working days.

### 6\. Refunds and Dispute Resolution

We handle refunds and disputes as follows:

- Submit a complaint or dispute along with relevant evidence via the app.
- Our support team will assess transaction records and supporting documents.
- We aim to resolve and communicate decisions within 7 business days.

**Note:** Refunds may be denied if there is evidence of fraud, abuse, or violation of our terms.

### 7\. Escrow Transaction Data Storage

We securely store the following escrow-related data:

- Transaction amount, sender/receiver details, date, status
- Order details and supporting documents
- In-app messages and timestamps

This data is encrypted and retained to fulfill regulatory, legal, and accountability obligations.

### 8\. Data Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
| --- | --- |
| Transaction & Order Data | 6 years |
| KYC Documents | 7 years after account closure |
| Support & Communication Logs | 2 years |
| Profile Data | Until account is deleted or inactive for 24 months |

You may request early deletion of your data, unless we are legally required to retain it.

### 9\. Your Rights Under the NDPR

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Request correction or deletion
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Object to certain types of processing
- Lodge a complaint with the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA)

To exercise your rights, contact us at **hello@demat.shop.** We may require identity verification before fulfilling your request.

### 10\. Data Security Measures

We protect your data through:

- End-to-end encryption
- Role-based access controls
- Two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Secure cloud storage
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

These measures are regularly reviewed and updated to stay ahead of threats.

### 11\. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies for:

- Session management
- User preferences
- Website analytics ( via Google Analytics)

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Third-party cookies are subject to their respective privacy policies.

### 12\. International Data Transfers

We currently store and process all personal data within Nigeria. If we need to transfer data internationally in the future, we will:

- Apply NDPR-compliant safeguards
- Obtain necessary approvals
- Inform users in advance and seek appropriate consent

### 13\. Accountability and Compliance

To ensure ongoing compliance with NDPR:

- We conduct regular data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)
- Employees receive mandatory privacy training
- Privacy risks are reviewed regularly, and controls updated accordingly

### 14\. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If we make significant changes:

- We will notify users at least 30 days in advance
- Updated policies will be published on our website and app

Please review this policy regularly to stay informed.
