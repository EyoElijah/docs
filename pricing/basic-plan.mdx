The Basic plan at ₦4,000/month is designed for growing sellers who need a professional storefront and marketplace visibility. It gives you everything you need to present your brand credibly, attract buyers through search, and manage your product catalog efficiently — all at an accessible price point.

## What's Included

Subscribing to the Basic plan unlocks the following features:

- **Professional online storefront** — A fully functional store page representing your brand on Demat.
- **Search engine optimization (SEO)** — Your store and products are optimized to appear in Google and other search results.
- **Store visibility on Demat Marketplace** — Your store is listed and discoverable by buyers browsing the Demat marketplace.
- **Store design customization** — Personalize your store's colors, layout, and branding to match your business identity.
- **Upload up to 100 products** — Build a comprehensive catalog with up to one hundred active product listings.
- **Manage product options** — Add sizes, colors, and other variants so buyers can choose exactly what they need.
- **AI-powered product recommendations** — Demat's AI surfaces your products to relevant buyers automatically.
- **Order management dashboard** — Track, update, and fulfill all your orders from a single view.
- **Free NGN Business Bank Account** — Receive a free Nigerian business bank account to separate personal and business finances.
- **Customer reviews & ratings** — Build trust by collecting and displaying genuine buyer feedback on your store.
- **Secure payment processing** — All transactions are protected by Demat's escrow-backed payment system.
- **Mobile-optimized store** — Your storefront looks and works great on smartphones and tablets.
- **Dedicated store link** — Share your unique store URL (e.g., demat.shop/yourstore) anywhere.
- **Email & chat support** — Reach the Demat support team via email or in-app chat when you need help.

## Pricing

The Basic plan costs **₦4,000/month**. Choose the billing cycle that works best for you:

| Billing Cycle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | Pay ₦4,000 each month. Cancel anytime. |
| **Bi-Annual** | Pay every six months at a reduced rate. |
| **Annual** | Pay once a year for the greatest savings. |

Longer billing cycles offer savings compared to the equivalent monthly total.

## Subscribe to Basic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Settings">
    Go to **Account → Subscription** in your Demat dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Basic Plan">
    Click on **Basic Plan** from the available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Billing Cycle">
    Select **Monthly**, **Bi-Annual**, or **Annual**. Longer cycles come with savings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Confirm and complete your payment. Your Basic plan activates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
