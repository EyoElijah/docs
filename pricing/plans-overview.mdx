<Info>

</Info>

## Billing Cycles

Demat gives you flexibility in how you pay. Choose the billing cycle that suits your budget:

- **Monthly** — Pay month-to-month with no long-term commitment.
- **Bi-Annual** — Pay every six months and save compared to the monthly rate.
- **Annual** — Pay once a year for the greatest savings overall.

Longer billing cycles offer discounts over the equivalent monthly total. You can select or change your billing cycle at any time from **Account → Subscription**.

<Note>
  All plans include escrow-backed payment protection, order management, and your dedicated store link (e.g., demat.shop/yourstore). In-app customer chat is available on the Free and Premium plans.
</Note>

## Upgrading Your Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Settings">
    Go to **Account → Subscription** in your Demat dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Plan">
    Choose the plan you want to upgrade to — Basic or Premium.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Billing Cycle">
    Select **Monthly**, **Bi-Annual**, or **Annual** based on your preference. Longer cycles unlock additional savings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Confirm and complete your payment. Your upgrade takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start on the Free plan to get familiar with Demat, then upgrade when you're ready to grow. You can move between plans at any time.
</Tip>
