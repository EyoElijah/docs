The Free plan lets you start selling on Demat immediately with no subscription cost. You get a dedicated store link, in-app chat, and the core tools to record orders and track your activity — everything you need to take your first steps as a seller on Demat.

## What's Included

The Free plan gives you access to the following features at no charge:

- **Create Memo** — Draft and save notes or memos within your store dashboard.
- **Record Order** — Log and track orders manually as they come in.
- **View Analytics** — Access your store's performance overview and key metrics.
- **View Transactions** — Review your full transaction history at a glance.
- **View Orders** — Monitor the status of all your orders in one place.
- **View Customers** — See who's buying from your store and manage customer records.
- **View Products** — Browse and manage your listed products.
- **Dedicated Store Link** — Share your personal storefront URL (e.g., demat.shop/yourstore) with buyers directly.
- **In-App Customer Chat** — Communicate with buyers in real time for negotiation, questions, and pickup coordination.

## Perfect For

The Free plan is a great fit if you are:

- An **individual listing occasional items** — second-hand goods, handmade crafts, or personal belongings you no longer need.
- A **new seller testing the platform** before committing to a paid subscription.
- **Anyone who wants a personal store link** to share on social media or with friends and family.

## Individual Listings

You can also list individual products on the Demat marketplace without a store plan — ideal for selling one-off items without setting up a full storefront. Your listing appears in the marketplace and is visible to buyers browsing that category, even without an active subscription. This is the easiest way to make your first sale on Demat.

<Note>
  The Free plan does not include marketplace SEO, store design customization, or product variant management. Upgrade to Basic or Premium to access those features and grow your store's reach.
</Note>
