The Premium plan at ₦8,500/month is built for larger businesses that need unlimited products, team management, and advanced analytics. Whether you're running a high-volume catalog, managing a team of staff, or need detailed performance data to make smarter decisions, Premium gives you the full power of the Demat platform.

## What's Included

The Premium plan includes everything in Basic, plus:

- **Upload unlimited products** — Remove the 100-product cap and list your entire catalog without restrictions.
- **Add product videos** — Showcase your products with video demonstrations to increase buyer confidence.
- **Bulk import & export of products (CSV/Excel)** — Add or update hundreds of products at once using spreadsheet templates.
- **Export order data for offline analysis** — Download your full order history in CSV/Excel format for reporting or accounting.
- **Add multiple store managers/staff accounts** — Invite team members with role-based access to help manage your store.
- **Advanced analytics & performance insights** — Dive deeper into revenue trends, customer behavior, and product-level data.
- **In-app customer chat** — Communicate directly with buyers for negotiation, questions, and order coordination.
- **Inventory sync** — Keep stock levels accurate across large catalogs and high order volumes.
- **Shipping integrations** *(coming soon)* — Connect with shipping providers directly from your Demat dashboard.
- **Downloadable invoice for each order** — Generate a PDF invoice for every completed order automatically.

## Key Highlights

<Accordion title="Unlimited Products">
  Remove the 100-product limit entirely. List your full catalog — whether that's 200 products or 20,000 — without any restrictions. Ideal for stores with large or frequently updated inventories.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Staff Accounts & Team Management">
  Add store managers and staff members with role-based access controls. Each team member gets their own login, so you can delegate order management, inventory updates, and customer service without sharing your personal account credentials. Collaboration becomes seamless as your team grows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Advanced Analytics">
  Go beyond basic dashboards. Premium analytics give you revenue trends over time, customer behavior patterns, product-level performance data, and actionable insights to optimize your pricing, inventory, and marketing decisions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bulk Import & Export">
  Import hundreds of products at once using Demat's CSV or Excel templates — no manual entry required. Export your full product catalog or complete order history for offline analysis, stock reconciliation, or accounting purposes. Perfect for sellers migrating from another platform or managing inventory in external systems.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Downloadable Invoices">
  Automatically generate a professional PDF invoice for every completed order. Use invoices for accounting records, tax filing, or to share with customers who need documentation of their purchase. All invoices are available to download directly from your order dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Inventory Sync">
  Keep your stock levels accurate at scale. Inventory sync automatically updates product availability as orders come in, preventing overselling and keeping your catalog up to date — essential for sellers managing large catalogs with high daily order volumes.
</Accordion>

## Pricing

The Premium plan costs **₦8,500/month**. Choose the billing cycle that works best for your business:

| Billing Cycle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | Pay ₦8,500 each month. Full flexibility. |
| **Bi-Annual** | Pay every six months at a reduced rate. |
| **Annual** | Pay once a year for the greatest savings. |

<Tip>
  The annual plan offers the best value. Calculate your exact savings by visiting **Account → Subscription** and comparing the billing options side by side.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Shipping integrations are coming soon to the Premium plan. Once available, you'll be able to connect with shipping providers directly from your Demat dashboard at no extra cost.
</Note>

## Subscribe to Premium

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subscription Settings">
    Go to **Account → Subscription** in your Demat dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Premium Plan">
    Click on **Premium Plan** from the available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Billing Cycle">
    Select **Monthly**, **Bi-Annual**, or **Annual**. Longer cycles unlock the best savings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    Confirm and complete your payment. Your Premium plan activates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
