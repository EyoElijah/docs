Whether you're an individual listing a few items or a growing business with hundreds of products, Demat gives you the tools to sell with confidence and reach buyers near you.

## How Demat Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Free Account">
    Sign up at [demat.shop](https://www.demat.shop) with your email. No credit card needed. Your account works for both buying and selling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Store or Browse the Marketplace">
    As a seller, add products or services to your branded storefront and share your unique store link. As a buyer, explore thousands of listings across categories like Fashion, Gadgets, Beauty, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat, Agree & Transact">
    Message sellers directly in the app. Negotiate, ask questions, and arrange pickup — all without leaving Demat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure Escrow Checkout">
    Buyer funds are held in escrow until you confirm delivery. Once satisfied, the seller receives payment. Both sides are protected.
  </Step>
</Steps>
