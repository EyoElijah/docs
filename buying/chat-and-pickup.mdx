Demat's built-in chat lets you communicate directly with sellers without leaving the app. Whether you want to ask a quick question before buying, negotiate on price, or lock down the details of a pickup, the in-app messaging system keeps everything in one place — and creates a record that protects you if anything goes wrong later.

## Starting a Chat

From any product listing or seller store page, tap the **chat icon** to open a conversation with the seller. You can start a chat before or after placing an order. Common reasons to reach out include:

- **Asking product questions** — get clarity on size, condition, specifications, or availability before committing
- **Negotiating price** — propose an offer or discuss a bundle deal directly in chat
- **Requesting additional photos** — ask the seller for more angles, close-ups, or proof of condition
- **Discussing pickup arrangements** — agree on a meeting point, preferred time, or delivery option

<Tip>
  Keep all communication within Demat's chat. Every message is logged and timestamped, which means your conversation can be used as evidence if a dispute is ever raised.
</Tip>

## Arranging Pickup

Once your order is confirmed and funds are in escrow, use chat to finalise the handover details. Follow these tips for a smooth and safe pickup experience:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agree on location and time before leaving home">
    Confirm the exact meeting point and a specific time with the seller over chat before you travel. Avoid vague arrangements like "somewhere in Ikeja" — pin a landmark or send a maps link for clarity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Choose a public, well-lit meeting place">
    For in-person pickups, opt for busy, visible locations such as a shopping mall entrance, bank forecourt, or filling station. Avoid secluded areas, especially for high-value items.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inspect the item before confirming delivery">
    When you receive the item, check it thoroughly against the listing — verify the condition, quantity, and any agreed specifications. Do this before tapping **Confirm Delivery** in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Don't confirm delivery until you're satisfied">
    Confirming delivery releases the escrowed funds to the seller immediately. Only tap **Confirm Delivery** once you have the item in hand and are happy with it. If something is wrong, raise a dispute instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Chat for Service Bookings

When you book a professional service through Demat — such as a repair, cleaning, tutoring session, or design job — use in-app chat to handle the pre-appointment coordination. Before your appointment, use chat to:

- **Confirm the date, time, and duration** of the service
- **Share your address or location** so the provider can plan their route
- **Clarify the scope of work** — be specific about what's included so there are no surprises on the day
- **Send any preparatory instructions** the provider may need (e.g., parking details, materials to bring)

<Info>
  Service providers can also use the Demat appointment booking system to send you a booking confirmation. Check your notifications for any scheduling updates from your provider.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Be cautious of sellers or service providers who ask to move the conversation to WhatsApp, Telegram, or any other external platform. Switching off-platform removes your protection — stay in Demat chat for all transaction-related communication.
</Warning>
