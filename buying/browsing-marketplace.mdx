The Demat marketplace connects you with sellers across Nigeria offering everything from everyday fashion to professional services. Whether you know exactly what you're looking for or you're just exploring, the marketplace gives you multiple ways to discover products, compare sellers, and shop with confidence — all in one place.

## Browsing Stores

Every seller on Demat has a dedicated storefront page where all their active listings, ratings, and contact options are grouped in one place. Access any store directly at **demat.shop/[storename]** — for example, `demat.shop/adaeze-fashion`. From a store page you can browse the seller's full catalogue, read customer reviews, check their completed order history, and open a chat before making a purchase.

## Finding Items Near You

Demat displays the approximate distance between you and each seller so you can prioritise listings that are convenient for pickup. Every listing card shows the product price, the seller's name, and how far away they are from your current location. Choosing a nearby seller can reduce delivery time and make it easier to arrange a quick in-person handover.

## Search and Filters

Use the search bar at the top of the marketplace to find specific products by name, keyword, or brand. Once you have results, refine them using the available filters:

- **Category** — narrow results to a single product category
- **Price range** — set a minimum and maximum price to match your budget
- **Location** — show only sellers within a specific area or city

Combining search terms with filters gives you the most relevant results and helps you find the right item faster.

<Tip>
  Look for sellers with strong ratings and a healthy completed-order history. High completion rates and positive reviews are a reliable sign of a trustworthy seller.
</Tip>
