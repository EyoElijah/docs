Demat's escrow system is the core trust mechanism behind every transaction on the platform. Instead of paying a seller directly and hoping the item arrives as described, your funds are held securely by Demat the moment you place an order. The seller only receives payment after you confirm that you've received your item and are satisfied. This means neither side takes on unnecessary risk — buyers are protected from non-delivery, and sellers are guaranteed payment once their obligation is fulfilled.

## How Escrow Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buyer funds their wallet">
    You add money to your Demat wallet via Paystack. A 1% deposit fee applies, capped at ₦300. Your wallet balance must cover the full order amount before you can proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer places an order — funds enter escrow">
    When you confirm your order, the purchase amount is immediately moved from your wallet into Demat's escrow. The funds are held there — neither you nor the seller can access them during this period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller fulfils the order">
    The seller prepares and delivers the item (or provides the service). Once done, the seller marks the order as **Delivered** in the app, triggering a notification to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer confirms delivery">
    You inspect the item and confirm within **48–72 hours** that you're satisfied. Tap **Confirm Delivery** in your order to signal that everything is in order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escrow releases funds to the seller">
    Once you confirm delivery, Demat releases the escrowed amount to the seller's wallet, minus the **3% seller fee**. The transaction is now complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Escrow Protects Against

Demat escrow provides meaningful protection for both parties in every transaction.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Protection for Buyers" icon="user-shield">
    If the seller doesn't deliver your item, or the item you receive doesn't match the listing, you can raise a dispute and have your funds returned. Your money never goes directly to the seller until you approve it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Protection for Sellers" icon="store">
    Once a buyer confirms delivery, payment is guaranteed. Sellers don't have to worry about chargebacks or buyers claiming non-receipt after a successful handover.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Raising a Dispute

If something goes wrong with your order — the item wasn't delivered, is significantly different from the listing, or is damaged — you can raise a formal dispute through the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order in the app">
    Go to your **Orders** tab and select the relevant order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Raise Dispute'">
    You'll find this option on the order detail screen. It becomes available once the seller has marked the order as delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your reason and add evidence">
    Choose the reason that best describes your issue (e.g., item not received, item doesn't match listing, item is damaged). Attach supporting evidence — photos, screenshots of your chat, or any relevant documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demat's support team reviews your case">
    Our team will examine the evidence submitted by both parties and may reach out to you for additional information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the decision">
    Demat will communicate the outcome of the dispute **within 7 business days**. If the decision is in your favour, a refund will be processed to your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refunds

If a dispute is resolved in your favour, or if you cancel an order before it is fulfilled, your escrowed funds will be returned to your Demat wallet within **3–5 business days** of the resolution or cancellation.

<Warning>
  Escrow protection only applies to transactions completed through Demat's in-app payment system. If you pay a seller outside the app — by bank transfer, cash, or any other method — Demat has no ability to hold, freeze, or recover those funds on your behalf.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Refunds may be denied if the investigation finds evidence of fraud, abuse, or a violation of Demat's Terms of Service. Submitting false dispute claims may result in account suspension.
</Note>
