Buying on Demat is designed to be straightforward and secure. Every purchase is backed by Demat's escrow system, which holds your payment until you confirm that you've received your item — so your money is never at risk. Follow the steps below to go from browsing to a completed transaction.

## Placing an Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the product you want">
    Browse the marketplace by category, use the search bar to look up a specific item, or navigate directly to a seller's store at `demat.shop/[storename]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the listing">
    Check the product photos, description, price, and seller rating carefully before proceeding. If you have questions, tap the chat icon to ask the seller directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet if needed">
    Go to **Wallet → Fund Wallet** and add funds via Paystack. A deposit fee of **1% applies, capped at ₦300**. Make sure your wallet balance covers the full order amount before checking out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Buy Now' or make an offer">
    Tap **Buy Now** to purchase at the listed price. If you'd like to negotiate, use the **Make an Offer** option instead. You can add a note to the seller when submitting your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your order">
    Review your order summary and confirm. Your funds move from your wallet into Demat's escrow — they are held securely there until you confirm delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat with the seller">
    Use Demat's in-app chat to coordinate pickup — agree on a location, date, and time that works for both of you. Keep all communication inside the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your item">
    Meet the seller (or receive the delivery) and inspect the item thoroughly before acknowledging receipt. Make sure everything matches the listing description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery in the app">
    Once you're satisfied, go to your order and tap **Confirm Delivery**. This releases the escrowed funds to the seller and closes the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only pay through Demat's in-app payment system. Transactions completed outside the app — via bank transfer, cash, or third-party apps — are **not covered by escrow protection**, and Demat cannot intervene if something goes wrong.
</Warning>

## Making an Offer

If the listed price doesn't match your budget, you can negotiate by submitting an offer below the asking price. The seller will receive a notification and can **accept**, **counter with a new price**, or **decline** your offer. If the seller counters, you'll be notified and can choose to accept or continue negotiating through chat.

<Info>
  After receiving an item, you have **48–72 hours** to confirm or reject delivery. If you do not respond within this window, the platform may automatically release the escrowed funds to the seller. Always confirm or raise a dispute promptly.
</Info>

## Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order at any point before the seller marks it as fulfilled. To cancel, open the order in your account and tap **Cancel Order**. Escrowed funds will be returned to your Demat wallet within **3–5 business days**.

<Note>
  Once a seller has marked an order as delivered, cancellation is no longer available. Use the dispute process instead if you have an issue with a delivered order.
</Note>
