The Demat wallet is your central financial hub on the platform. Use it to fund purchases, receive earnings from completed sales, and withdraw money directly to your bank account — all in one place, secured by escrow and compliant with Nigerian data protection regulations.

## Wallet Balances

Your wallet displays two distinct balances at all times, each serving a different purpose:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Available Balance" icon="wallet">
    Funds that are ready to use immediately. You can spend this balance on purchases or withdraw it to your bank account at any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Escrow Balance" icon="lock">
    Funds currently locked in active transactions — either as a buyer waiting for delivery confirmation, or as a seller awaiting release after fulfillment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Your escrow balance is protected — it cannot be withdrawn until the transaction is fully resolved, ensuring neither party can access the funds prematurely.
</Info>

## How Funds Move

Every naira in your wallet follows a clear, traceable path from deposit to withdrawal. Here's how money flows through the system:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fund Your Wallet">
    You deposit funds via Paystack bank transfer. Your **Available Balance** increases by the deposited amount (minus the 1% processing fee, capped at ₦300).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place an Order">
    When you purchase an item, the order amount moves from your **Available Balance** into **Escrow**. The funds are now held securely and protected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer Confirms Delivery">
    Once you confirm that your order has been received and is satisfactory, the escrowed funds are released to the seller's **Available Balance** — minus the 3% seller fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw Earnings">
    The seller withdraws their earnings to a linked bank account. Their **Available Balance** decreases by the withdrawal amount plus the applicable withdrawal fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activating Your Wallet

Before you can make deposits or withdrawals, you must activate your wallet using your Bank Verification Number (BVN).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Navigate to **Account Settings** in the Demat app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Wallet">
    Select **Wallet**, then tap **Activate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your BVN">
    Provide your 11-digit BVN when prompted. Your BVN is encrypted and stored securely in line with NDPR requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    Once verified, your wallet is fully activated and ready for deposits and withdrawals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can browse the marketplace and set up your store without activating your wallet. However, you'll need an active wallet to make purchases or receive payments from buyers.
</Note>
