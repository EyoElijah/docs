Demat's escrow system and dispute resolution process protect both buyers and sellers when something goes wrong. Whether you need to cancel an order before fulfillment, report a problem with a delivery, or respond to a dispute raised against you, this guide walks you through every step.

## Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order before the seller has fulfilled it. Once the seller ships or hands over the item, cancellation is no longer available — use the dispute process instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Your Active Orders">
    Open the Demat app and navigate to **Orders** to view your active purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Order">
    Tap the order you want to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Cancel Order'">
    Select **Cancel Order** from the order detail screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide a Reason">
    Choose a cancellation reason from the list — for example, change of mind, incorrect order, or found a better price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund to Wallet">
    Your escrowed funds are returned to your wallet within **3–7 working days** of the cancellation being processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Cancelled order funds are returned to your Demat wallet Available Balance, not directly to your bank account. From there, you can withdraw to your bank or use the funds for another purchase.
</Info>

## Raising a Dispute

If you receive an item that doesn't match the listing description, arrives damaged, or the seller fails to fulfill the order, raise a dispute to protect your funds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Order">
    Navigate to **Orders** and open the relevant order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Raise Dispute'">
    Select **Raise Dispute** from the order detail screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Reason">
    Choose the reason that best describes the issue — for example: item not as described, item not received, or seller unresponsive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Supporting Evidence">
    Attach any relevant evidence to strengthen your case. This can include photos of the item received, screenshots of your in-app chat with the seller, or any other documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the Dispute">
    Review your submission and tap **Submit**. Demat's support team will take it from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dispute Resolution Process

Once a dispute is submitted, Demat's support team manages the process from review through to decision.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Case Review">
    The Demat support team reviews all submitted information, including your evidence, the seller's listing, and any chat history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both Parties Contacted">
    Either or both parties may be contacted by the support team for additional information or clarification. Respond promptly to avoid delays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decision Communicated">
    A decision is communicated to both parties within **7 business days** of the dispute being submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund Processed">
    If the dispute is resolved in the buyer's favor, the refund is processed and credited to the buyer's wallet within **3–5 business days** of the decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Refunds may be denied if there is evidence of fraud, abuse, or violation of Demat's terms of service. All decisions made by the support team are final.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Transactions made outside the Demat app — such as payments sent directly to a seller's personal bank account or via third-party transfer — are not eligible for dispute resolution or refunds. Always transact within the app to stay protected.
</Note>

## Seller Dispute Outcomes

If a buyer raises a dispute against you as a seller, cooperate fully with the Demat support team throughout the review process.

<Accordion title="What should I do if a dispute is raised against me?">
  Respond promptly when the support team contacts you. Gather and submit any evidence that supports your case, including:

  - Photos of the item before it was shipped or handed over
  - Proof of dispatch or delivery (tracking numbers, receipts)
  - Screenshots of in-app chat with the buyer showing agreed terms
  - Any other documentation relevant to the transaction

  The support team reviews evidence from both parties impartially. Decisions are communicated within 7 business days and are final.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my escrow funds during a dispute?">
  Funds held in escrow remain locked for the duration of the dispute. They are neither released to you nor returned to the buyer until the support team issues a decision. This protects both parties equally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I appeal a dispute decision?">
  Decisions made by the Demat support team are final. If you believe there has been an error, contact the support team at hello@demat.shop or call +234 810 535 7067 to request a case review.
</Accordion>
