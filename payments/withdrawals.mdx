Once you've made sales on Demat, withdraw your earnings from your wallet to your Nigerian bank account at any time. Withdrawals are straightforward, and a small flat fee applies depending on the amount you withdraw. Make sure your bank account details are set up before you request a withdrawal.

## How to Withdraw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Wallet → Withdraw">
    Open the Demat app, navigate to your **Wallet**, and tap **Withdraw**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Amount">
    Type in the amount you want to withdraw. The applicable fee will be displayed before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Bank Account">
    Choose the bank account you want to receive the funds. If you haven't added one yet, go to **Account Settings → Bank Details** and add your account number and bank name before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Withdrawal">
    Review the amount, fee, and destination bank account, then confirm the withdrawal request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds Arrive in Your Bank">
    Your funds are processed and arrive in your bank account within the standard processing window. Most withdrawals complete within one business day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You must have added and verified a bank account before making a withdrawal. Navigate to **Account Settings → Bank Details** to add your account.
</Note>

## Withdrawal Fees

Demat charges a small flat fee per withdrawal based on the amount. These fees are deducted from your wallet at the time of withdrawal.

| Withdrawal Amount | Fee |
|---|---|
| ₦5,000 or less | ₦10 |
| ₦5,001 – ₦50,000 | ₦25 |
| Above ₦50,000 | ₦50 |

<Info>
  Withdrawal fees are flat — not percentage-based — so larger withdrawals are proportionally cheaper. Withdrawing ₦100,000 costs just ₦50 in fees.
</Info>

## Seller Payout

When a buyer confirms delivery of their order, the escrow holding the transaction funds is released. Your payout is credited to your **Available Balance** automatically, with the 3% seller fee deducted at that point.

**Example payout calculation:**

| Sale Price | Seller Fee (3%) | Amount Credited to Wallet |
|---|---|---|
| ₦5,000 | ₦150 | ₦4,850 |
| ₦10,000 | ₦300 | ₦9,700 |
| ₦50,000 | ₦1,500 | ₦48,500 |
| ₦100,000 | ₦3,000 | ₦97,000 |

<Tip>
  Regularly withdraw your earnings to your bank rather than leaving large sums in your wallet. It's good financial hygiene and keeps your business cash flow healthy.
</Tip>

## Free NGN Business Bank Account (Basic & Premium)

Sellers on the **Basic** and **Premium** plans receive a free Nigerian Naira (NGN) business bank account as part of their subscription. This account is useful for separating your business income from your personal finances, making bookkeeping and tax reporting simpler.
