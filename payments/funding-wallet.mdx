Fund your Demat wallet to start buying products, booking services, or as a business owner to hold operational funds within the platform. Deposits are processed instantly through Paystack, Nigeria's trusted payment infrastructure, and your Available Balance updates as soon as the transfer is confirmed.

## How to Fund Your Wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Demat App and Go to Wallet">
    Launch the Demat app and tap **Wallet** from your dashboard or the bottom navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Fund Wallet'">
    Select the **Fund Wallet** option to begin the deposit process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Amount">
    Type in the amount you want to deposit in Naira (₦). Make sure to account for the Paystack processing fee so your Available Balance reaches your intended amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Bank Transfer via Paystack">
    You'll be redirected to a secure Paystack payment page. Follow the instructions to complete the bank transfer from your Nigerian bank account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit Confirmed">
    Once Paystack confirms the transfer, your **Available Balance** updates immediately. You're ready to buy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Deposit a small buffer above your intended purchase price to cover the Paystack processing fee. For example, if an item costs ₦20,000, add ₦200 (1% of ₦20,000) to your deposit — so deposit ₦20,200 — and your Available Balance will comfortably cover the purchase after the fee is deducted.
</Tip>

## Deposit Fees

Paystack charges a **1% processing fee** on all wallet deposits. This fee is capped at **₦300** regardless of how large your deposit is.

| Deposit Amount | Fee (1%) | Capped? | You Are Charged |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₦5,000 | ₦50 | No | ₦50 |
| ₦10,000 | ₦100 | No | ₦100 |
| ₦30,000 | ₦300 | Yes (cap reached) | ₦300 |
| ₦50,000 | ₦500 → ₦300 | Yes (capped) | ₦300 |
| ₦100,000 | ₦1,000 → ₦300 | Yes (capped) | ₦300 |

<Info>
  The deposit fee is charged by Paystack as a payment processing charge, not by Demat directly. Large deposits benefit from the ₦300 cap.
</Info>

## Minimum Deposit

Demat supports small deposits, making it accessible whether you're topping up a small amount or funding a large business purchase. There is no high minimum deposit requirement — you can fund your wallet with whatever amount you need.

<Warning>
  Only fund your wallet through the official Demat app using the Paystack payment page. Never transfer money directly to a seller's personal bank account — such transactions are not protected by escrow and are not eligible for dispute resolution or refunds.
</Warning>
