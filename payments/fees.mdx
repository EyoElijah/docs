Demat charges minimal fees to keep the platform running and payment infrastructure secure. There are no hidden charges — every fee is clearly defined, automatically deducted, and shown to you before you confirm any transaction. Here's a full breakdown of every charge you may encounter as a buyer or seller.

## Fee Summary

| Service | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet Deposit | 1% (max ₦300) | Paystack processing charge |
| Withdrawal (₦5,000 or less) | ₦10 flat | Deducted from wallet at withdrawal |
| Withdrawal (₦5,001 – ₦50,000) | ₦25 flat | Deducted from wallet at withdrawal |
| Withdrawal (above ₦50,000) | ₦50 flat | Deducted from wallet at withdrawal |
| Completed Transaction (Seller) | 3% of payout | Deducted at escrow release |

<Info>
  All fees are deducted automatically at the relevant step — you never need to calculate or pay them separately. The exact fee is always shown before you confirm.
</Info>

## Subscription Plan Fees

Your subscription plan unlocks additional storefront features and is billed separately from transaction fees. Choosing a paid plan does not reduce or waive transaction fees.

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₦0 | Individuals getting started |
| Basic | ₦4,000/month | Growing sellers needing more tools |
| Premium | ₦8,500/month | Businesses scaling with full features |

<Note>
  Plans can be billed monthly, bi-annually, or annually. Annual billing may offer savings compared to month-to-month pricing.
</Note>

<Accordion title="What does each plan include?">
  **Free** — Create memos, record orders, view analytics, transactions, orders, customers, and products. Includes a dedicated store link at `demat.shop/[storename]` and in-app chat.

  **Basic** — Everything in Free, plus up to 100 products, SEO tools, store design customization, AI product recommendations, a free NGN Business Bank Account, and customer reviews & ratings.

  **Premium** — Everything in Basic, plus unlimited products, product videos, bulk CSV/Excel import and export, multiple staff accounts with role-based access, advanced analytics, inventory sync, and downloadable invoices.
</Accordion>

## No Buyer Transaction Fee

Buyers are not charged a transaction fee on purchases. The only charge a buyer may encounter is the **1% Paystack deposit fee** (capped at ₦300) when adding funds to their wallet. Once funds are in the wallet, buying costs nothing extra.

<Tip>
  As a buyer, deposit slightly more than your intended purchase amount to cover the Paystack fee, so your Available Balance fully covers the item price.
</Tip>
