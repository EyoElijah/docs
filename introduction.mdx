Demat is Africa's all-in-one marketplace platform connecting buyers, sellers, and service providers across Nigeria and beyond. Whether you're clearing out items at home or running a growing business, Demat gives you the tools to list, sell, and get paid with confidence. Every transaction is protected by an escrow-backed payment system that holds funds safely until delivery is confirmed, while built-in in-app chat keeps buyers and sellers in sync every step of the way. From a dedicated storefront link to real-time sales analytics, Demat brings professional commerce tools within reach of everyone.

## What is Demat?

Demat is a marketplace platform built for Africa — designed so that anyone can buy or sell without friction. It serves four distinct groups of users, each with purpose-built tools:

- **Individual sellers** can list a single item for free without creating a business account. Snap a photo, set a price, and your listing is live in minutes.
- **Businesses** can run fully branded storefronts complete with inventory management, staff accounts, advanced analytics, and a custom store link at `demat.shop/[storename]`.
- **Buyers** can browse products and services across eight categories, communicate directly with sellers via in-app chat, and pay confidently knowing escrow protection keeps their funds safe until delivery is confirmed.
- **Service providers** can advertise professional services, set availability, and accept appointment bookings directly through the platform.

## Who Is Demat For?

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Individual Sellers">
    You don't need a business account to start selling on Demat. If you have items you want to sell — second-hand clothing, a spare gadget, homemade goods — you can list them for free on the Free plan. Snap a photo, write a short description, set your price, and your listing goes live instantly on the marketplace. Buyers can find your item through search or category browsing, message you through in-app chat to negotiate or arrange pickup, and pay securely through escrow. You only receive payment once the buyer confirms delivery, giving both sides peace of mind.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Business Owners">
    Demat gives growing businesses a complete commerce toolkit. Upgrade to the **Basic plan (₦4,000/month)** to unlock up to 100 product listings, SEO tools, store design customisation, AI product recommendations, customer reviews and ratings, and a free NGN Business Bank Account. Step up to the **Premium plan (₦8,500/month)** for unlimited products, product videos, bulk CSV/Excel import and export, multiple staff accounts with role-based access, advanced analytics, inventory sync, and downloadable invoices. Every business gets a branded storefront at `demat.shop/[storename]` — a shareable link you can promote anywhere.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buyers">
    Buying on Demat is straightforward and secure. Browse thousands of listings across eight categories — from fashion and gadgets to property and professional services — or use search to find exactly what you need. When you're ready to buy, your payment is held in escrow and only released to the seller after you confirm that the item arrived as described. Use in-app chat to ask the seller questions, negotiate a price, or coordinate a pickup time before you commit. If something goes wrong, Demat's dispute resolution process works to resolve issues within 7 business days, with refunds processed in 3–5 business days after a decision is reached.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Marketplace Categories

Demat organises listings into eight core categories, making it easy for buyers to browse and for sellers to reach the right audience:

- **Fashion** — clothing, footwear, accessories, and apparel
- **Gadgets** — phones, electronics, computers, and tech accessories
- **Property** — real estate listings, rentals, and land
- **Automobile** — cars, motorcycles, spare parts, and vehicles
- **Houseware** — furniture, appliances, and home essentials
- **Services** — professional and skilled services for hire
- **Thrift** — pre-owned and second-hand items
- **Beauty** — skincare, haircare, cosmetics, and wellness products
