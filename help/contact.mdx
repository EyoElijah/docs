## For Disputes and Refunds

The fastest way to resolve a transaction issue is to raise a dispute directly through the Demat app. This automatically logs the case with full transaction context and puts you in the review queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your order">
    Open the Demat app and navigate to **Orders**. Find the relevant order and tap to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Raise Dispute'">
    Select **Raise Dispute** from the order actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your reason and evidence">
    Describe the issue clearly and attach any supporting evidence — photos, chat screenshots, or delivery records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for review">
    Demat's support team will review your case and contact both parties. Disputes are typically **resolved within 7 business days**. If approved, refunds are processed within **3–5 business days**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only disputes raised through the app are eligible for Demat's escrow-backed resolution process. Off-platform transactions cannot be reviewed or refunded.
</Note>
