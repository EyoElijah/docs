Demat Shop Enterprise is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you hold under the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)**. By using Demat, you consent to the practices described here.

<Note>
  This page summarises Demat's privacy practices in plain language. The full privacy policy is available at [demat.shop/marketplace/privacy](https://demat.shop/marketplace/privacy).
</Note>

## Data We Collect

Demat collects only the data necessary to operate the platform, verify identities, process transactions, and comply with Nigerian law.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Identification Data** | Full name, phone number, email address, gender, BVN* |
| **Transaction Data** | Wallet balance, payment method, deposit and withdrawal history, order records |
| **KYC & Compliance Data** | Government-issued ID (e.g. NIN slip, international passport, driver's licence), utility bill |
| **Technical Data** | IP address, device type and model, browser type, operating system, login timestamps |
| **Communication Data** | Support tickets, in-app chat logs, feedback submissions |

<Note>
  **BVN (Bank Verification Number)** is treated as sensitive personal data. It is encrypted at rest, stored on secure infrastructure, and collected solely for regulatory identity verification and wallet activation. Demat does not share your BVN with third parties except where required by Nigerian law or financial regulations.
</Note>

## How We Use Your Data

Demat uses your personal data for the following purposes:

- **Account creation and management** — verifying your identity and enabling platform access
- **Wallet transactions** — processing deposits, withdrawals, and peer-to-peer payments
- **Order fulfilment** — facilitating purchases, deliveries, and seller payouts
- **Dispute resolution** — reviewing transaction evidence and communication records to resolve conflicts
- **Customer support** — responding to inquiries, complaints, and technical issues
- **KYC and AML compliance** — meeting obligations under Nigerian financial regulations
- **Marketing and promotions** — sending relevant offers and platform updates (only with your consent; you may opt out at any time)
- **Platform analytics** — understanding usage patterns to improve the Demat experience

## Data Retention

Demat retains your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil its stated purpose or meet legal obligations.

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Transaction & Order Data | 6 years from the date of the transaction |
| KYC Documents | 7 years after account closure |
| Support & Communication Logs | 2 years from the date of the interaction |
| Profile Data | Until you delete your account, or 24 months after your last active session |

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised in accordance with NDPR guidelines.

## Your Rights Under NDPR

As a data subject under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access** — request a copy of the personal data Demat holds about you
- **Right to Rectification** — ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Right to Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
- **Right to Withdraw Consent** — withdraw consent for marketing or non-essential data processing at any time
- **Right to Object** — object to specific types of data processing, including profiling
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint** — report concerns to the **National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA)**, Nigeria's data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email **hello@demat.shop** with your full name, registered phone number, and the specific request. Demat will respond within **7 working days**.

## Data Security

Demat applies multiple layers of technical and organisational controls to protect your data:

- **End-to-end encryption** for sensitive data in transit and at rest
- **Role-based access controls** to limit internal access to personal data
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** for account and admin access
- **Secure cloud storage** with industry-standard data centre protections
- **Regular security audits** and vulnerability assessments

Despite these measures, no system is completely immune to risk. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, contact support immediately at hello@demat.shop or call +234 810 535 7067.

## Cookies

Demat uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

- **Session management** — keeping you logged in during your visit
- **User preferences** — remembering your settings and browsing choices
- **Analytics** — measuring platform usage and performance via Google Analytics

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Demat platform.
