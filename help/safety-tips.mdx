Demat's escrow system provides strong financial protection, but practicing good safety habits makes every transaction smoother and safer. Whether you're buying a gadget or selling fashion items, the tips below help you avoid common pitfalls and trade with confidence.

## Buyer Safety Tips

Follow these practices every time you make a purchase on Demat:

- **Always pay through the Demat app.** Never send money directly to a seller's personal bank account, mobile money, or any payment channel outside the platform. If something goes wrong with an off-platform payment, Demat cannot protect you.
- **Inspect before you confirm delivery.** Only tap "Confirm Delivery" after you have physically received and thoroughly inspected the item. Once you confirm, funds are released to the seller and the transaction is closed.
- **Keep all communication in-app.** Use Demat's in-app chat for every negotiation, agreement, and instruction. This creates a documented record you can reference in a dispute.
- **Choose safe pickup locations.** If collecting in person, meet in a public, well-lit location — shopping centres, bank lobbies, or busy commercial areas are ideal.
- **Verify the item matches the listing.** Before confirming, compare the item against the listing photos, description, and agreed specifications. If something is wrong, raise a dispute immediately — do not confirm delivery.
- **Check seller ratings and reviews.** Review a seller's transaction history, star ratings, and buyer feedback before placing an order. Established sellers with positive reviews offer greater confidence.

## Seller Safety Tips

Protect yourself and your goods with these habits:

- **Confirm escrow before releasing the item.** Only hand over an item after verifying in the app that the order is active and the buyer's funds are held in escrow. Never release goods on the promise of a payment.
- **Don't accept "pending payment" claims.** If a buyer tells you their payment is "pending," "processing," or "on the way," do not release the item. Funds must show as secured in the Demat escrow before you proceed.
- **Document the item's condition.** Take clear photos or a short video of the item — showing its condition and any serial numbers — immediately before handing it over. This protects you in the event of a dispute.
- **Keep your chat records.** Maintain a record of all agreements made with the buyer through in-app chat. Screenshots of agreed prices, delivery terms, or conditions can support your case if a dispute arises.
- **Meet buyers in safe, public locations.** When arranging in-person handovers, choose busy, well-lit public spaces and, where possible, bring a trusted person with you.

## Red Flags to Watch For

<Warning>
  Stop the transaction immediately and contact Demat support if you notice any of the following:

  - A buyer or seller asking you to complete the transaction **outside the Demat app**
  - Pressure to **confirm delivery before inspecting** the item
  - Requests to **share your personal banking details**, OTP, or password
  - Prices that seem **too good to be true** — counterfeit or stolen goods are often listed this way
  - **Urgency tactics** designed to rush you into skipping verification steps
  - Unsolicited links or requests to move to WhatsApp, Telegram, or another platform to "complete the deal"
</Warning>
