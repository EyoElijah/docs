## Eligibility

To use Demat, you must:

- Be at least **18 years of age**
- Reside in **Nigeria**
- Provide accurate registration information, including a valid phone number and email address
- Maintain only one personal account on the platform

Demat reserves the right to request identity verification at any time and to suspend accounts that do not meet these requirements.

## Buyer Responsibilities

As a buyer on Demat, you agree to:

- **Fund your wallet before placing an order.** Orders are confirmed only when payment is secured in Demat's escrow system.
- **Confirm or reject delivery within the designated window** (48–72 hours of receiving your order). Failure to act within this window may result in automatic release of funds to the seller.
- **Inspect items before confirming delivery.** Once you confirm, the transaction is considered complete and funds are released.
- **Use the platform honestly.** Do not raise false disputes, manipulate reviews, or exploit the escrow system.
- **Keep all transaction communication in-app.** Off-platform agreements are not covered by Demat's dispute resolution process.

## Seller Responsibilities

As a seller on Demat, you agree to:

- **Accurately describe your listings.** Product photos, descriptions, prices, and conditions must reflect the actual item.
- **Respond to offers and buyer messages promptly.** Consistent non-response may negatively affect your seller rating and account standing.
- **Fulfil accepted orders.** Once an order is confirmed and escrow is secured, you are obligated to complete the transaction.
- **Only release items after confirming escrow.** Never hand over goods before verifying that funds are held in Demat's escrow.
- **Comply with applicable Nigerian laws** regarding the sale of goods and services.

## Payments and Fees

All payments on Demat are processed exclusively through the in-app wallet. The following fees apply:

| Fee Type | Rate / Amount |
|---|---|
| Wallet deposit fee | 1% of deposit amount (maximum ₦300) |
| Withdrawal — ₦5,000 and below | ₦10 flat fee |
| Withdrawal — ₦5,001 to ₦50,000 | ₦25 flat fee |
| Withdrawal — above ₦50,000 | ₦50 flat fee |
| Seller transaction fee | 3% deducted from each payout |

Demat does not accept or process payments made outside the platform. Off-platform transactions are not protected by escrow and are not eligible for dispute resolution.

## Escrow and Order Fulfillment

Demat uses an escrow-backed payment model to protect both buyers and sellers:

1. **Buyer funds wallet** — the buyer tops up their Demat wallet using Paystack bank transfer.
2. **Order placed** — when the buyer places an order, the payment is locked in Demat's escrow. The seller cannot access the funds yet.
3. **Seller fulfils the order** — the seller prepares and delivers or hands over the item.
4. **Buyer confirms delivery** — once satisfied, the buyer confirms receipt. Demat releases the funds (minus the 3% seller fee) to the seller's wallet.
5. **Dispute window** — if the buyer does not confirm or raise a dispute within the review window, the platform may release funds automatically per its fulfilment policy.

- Raise a dispute directly through the app on the relevant order page.

- Disputes are typically **resolved within 7 business days**.
- If a dispute cannot be resolved through Demat's internal process, it may be escalated to a **court of competent jurisdiction in Abuja, FCT, Nigeria**.
- All matters are governed by the **laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria**.
