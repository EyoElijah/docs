Find answers to the most common questions about using Demat. Whether you're a first-time buyer, a growing seller, or just exploring the platform, the sections below cover everything from account setup to payments and dispute resolution.

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Demat?">
    Demat is an African marketplace platform where individuals and businesses can list products and services, chat with buyers, and transact through an escrow-backed payment system. Every transaction is protected — buyers' funds are held securely until delivery is confirmed, so both sides can trade with confidence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can use Demat?">
    Anyone who is 18 years or older and resides in Nigeria can register and use Demat as a buyer or seller. You'll need a valid phone number and email address to create an account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Demat free to use?">
    Yes. Demat offers a Free plan at ₦0 — you can create an account, get a dedicated store link (demat.shop/[storename]), list individual products, and use in-app chat at no cost.

    If you want professional storefront features, two paid plans are available:

    | Plan | Price | Key Features |
    |---|---|---|
    | Basic | ₦4,000/month | Up to 100 products, SEO tools, store customization, AI recommendations, free NGN business bank account |
    | Premium | ₦8,500/month | Unlimited products, product videos, bulk import/export, staff accounts, advanced analytics |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Selling

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a registered business to sell on Demat?">
    No. You can list individual products without a business account — the Free plan is all you need to get started. If you want a full storefront with inventory management, analytics, and branding tools, upgrade to the Basic or Premium plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get paid?">
    When a buyer confirms delivery, Demat releases the escrowed funds to your wallet. A **3% seller fee** is deducted from the payout automatically. Once the funds are in your wallet, you can withdraw them to your bank account at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the seller fee?">
    Demat deducts **3%** from each completed transaction payout. For example:

    - A ₦10,000 sale → ₦9,700 credited to your wallet
    - A ₦50,000 sale → ₦48,500 credited to your wallet

    There are no hidden charges beyond this fee and standard withdrawal fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What marketplace categories can I sell in?">
    Demat supports listings across eight categories: **Fashion, Gadgets, Property, Automobile, Houseware, Services, Thrift,** and **Beauty**. Services sellers can also use Demat's built-in appointment and booking system.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Buying

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does escrow work?">
    When you place an order, your payment is held in Demat's secure escrow — not passed directly to the seller. The seller only receives payment after you confirm that you've received the item and are satisfied with it. If something goes wrong before you confirm, you can raise a dispute and the funds remain protected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't receive my order?">
    If your order doesn't arrive, raise a dispute through the app within the confirmation window. Demat's support team will review the case and work toward a resolution **within 7 business days**. If the dispute is resolved in your favour, a refund will be processed within **3–5 business days**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel an order?">
    Yes, you can cancel an order before it is fulfilled by the seller. Once cancelled, the escrowed funds will be returned to your wallet. If the order is already in progress, raise a dispute through the app — approved refunds are processed within **3–5 business days** of dispute resolution.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments & Wallet

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I fund my wallet?">
    Open the Demat app and navigate to **Wallet → Fund Wallet**. Deposits are processed via Paystack bank transfer. A **1% deposit fee (maximum ₦300)** applies to each top-up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a BVN?">
    Yes. Your Bank Verification Number (BVN) is required to activate wallet features, including deposits and withdrawals. Your BVN is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). It is used solely for regulatory identity verification and is never shared with third parties without your consent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do withdrawals take?">
    Withdrawals are typically processed within **1–2 business days** after submission, depending on your bank. The following withdrawal fees apply:

    | Amount | Fee |
    |---|---|
    | ₦5,000 and below | ₦10 |
    | ₦5,001 – ₦50,000 | ₦25 |
    | Above ₦50,000 | ₦50 |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
